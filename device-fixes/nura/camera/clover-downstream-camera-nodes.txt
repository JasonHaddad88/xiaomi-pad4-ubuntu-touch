clover downstream camera device tree, captured from the running Ubuntu Touch
kernel (4.4.153-HandsomeKernel+) on 24 October 2026, before Ubuntu Touch was
replaced. This is the sensor wiring a mainline V4L2 driver needs and it is not
recoverable from the boot image: the 111 DTBs appended to halium-boot.img carry
Qualcomm reference camera configs, not clover's.

GPIO references are <phandle pin flags>; phandle 0xec is the tlmm pinctrl, so
the middle value is the TLMM pin number (shown in decimal after each line).

Full tree archived at backups/clover-downstream-reference/
clover-downstream-devicetree.tar.gz (md5 c8a72aa76785120c3a0ccb9f6f64e7bf).
